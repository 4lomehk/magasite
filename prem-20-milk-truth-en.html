Preserving Pristine Living Nutrition · The Raw Milk Mechanism

Why does modern factory-processed milk pale in comparison to unpasteurized living milk?

1. Zero Heat Denaturation Raw milk refers to unpasteurized, unhomogenized milk. Without thermal shock, it fully retains active immunoglobulins, digestive lactase enzymes, fat-soluble vitamins (A, D3, K2), and beneficial probiotic cultures.
2. Easily Digestible & Anti-Inflammatory High-heat industrial pasteurization denatures whey proteins and caramelizes lactose, creating advanced glycation end-products. Raw milk lactose remains in its natural state, making assimilation smooth and bloating-free.
3. Hygiene Reality & HK Availability Raw milk requires flawless pasturing standards (zero tolerance for Listeria/E. coli). Hong Kong law does not permit commercial sale of unpasteurized milk; thus, 100% grass-fed low-temp whole milk is the best local fallback.
