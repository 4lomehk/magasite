Early Harvest 早摘嘅博弈 The Early Harvest Trade-Off

點解 9–10 月採收嘅油咁貴？因為橄欖仲係青色嗰陣，多酚含量係巔峰。雖然產量低，但藥用價值極高。隨著果實成熟變黑，入面嘅金液增加但多酚會急劇下跌。 Why are September–October harvest oils so expensive? The olive is still green — polyphenols are at peak concentration. Yes, yield is low, but medicinal value is highest. As the fruit ripens and blackens, oil content rises but polyphenol levels drop sharply.

數據軍備競賽：NMR vs HPLC The Data Arms Race: NMR vs HPLC

市面上品牌聲稱多酚過 2000mg，你要小心測試方法。NMR 測出嚟嘅數值通常比 HPLC 高出 40%–60%，好多品牌用 NMR 數據做 Marketing 係誤導。只信任 HPLC 或 LC-MS/MS 測法，呢啲先符合國際臨床標準。 Brands claiming polyphenol counts over 2000mg — watch the testing method. NMR readings typically run 40–60% higher than HPLC. Many brands use NMR for marketing — that's misleading. Trust only HPLC or LC-MS/MS methods; those meet international clinical standards.
